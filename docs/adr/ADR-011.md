# ADR-011: Views and mirroring model

- **Status**: Accepted
- **Date**: 2026-10-26

## Context
Multi-view characters (front, side, back) could not be modelled with one rig and one direction per animation (R-01), and baked mirror animations contradicted runtime `flip_h` (R-07).

## Decision
Animations are identified by unique `(group, direction)`. `Rig.views[]` hold per-view rest poses and variant sets; `directionFallback` maps missing directions (e.g. up to [up, side]). Mirroring is derived: an animation with `mirrorOf` stores only that reference (plus optional extra overrides), is mirrored in `renderFrame`, stores no PNGs in the cache, and is flipped at runtime in the engine including marker positions. Markers keep anatomical names (left/right do not swap).

## Consequences
- One authored side animation serves both directions, halving art and review effort.
- Asymmetric art needs an explicit non-mirrored animation or extra overrides.
- Invariant I6 (unique group/direction; mirrorOf targets a non-mirrored same-group animation) is enforced by `validate()`.
