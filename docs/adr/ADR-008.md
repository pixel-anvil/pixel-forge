# ADR-008: RotSprite, part variants and overrides (warping deferred)

- **Status**: Proposed
- **Date**: 2026-10-26

## Context
Pixel-aware rigging needs rotation that preserves the pixel look, plus a way to keep hand fixes when poses are re-rendered. Free-form mesh warping is a large scope increase with unclear payoff for small sprites. Status is Proposed until the RotSprite spike (T0.4) and the raster implementation (T1.6) provide evidence.

## Decision
Rotate parts with RotSprite (Scale2x x3, nearest rotate at x8, sample down, pivot tracked), choose among part variants (with hysteresis; an explicit key beats auto), and store manual touch-ups as part-relative overrides keyed by stable frame ids with a bbox+/-1 base hash for staleness. Overrides can also be 'final' (post-outline). Render order is fixed and deterministic. Mesh warping is deferred beyond M1.

## Consequences
- Quality rotation at small sizes with a bounded per-part cost target.
- Overrides survive re-posing; stale ones are flagged by lint and resolved with keep/discard/re-base.
- If the spike shows RotSprite is inadequate at some angles, variants carry those cases; this ADR is revised before acceptance.

## Evidence
T0.4 spike (`spikes/rotsprite/REPORT.md`): no new colours, exact pivot at 360/360 angles, 0.09 ms cold / 0.01 ms warm for 32×32, RotSprite better than nearest on parts ≥ 16 px. Variants are needed for hands, for feet beyond ±30°, and for heads. Status stays Proposed until G1, as planned.
