`walk` and `run` defaults follow drawn walk cycles (E7.5): the walk now starts its pelvis dip at 0 px (straight legs), lifts the swing foot by a 24th of the leg (0 on short legs) and strides three fifths of the leg; `run` lifts a quarter of the leg. Pass `bob`, `lift` or `strideLength` to get the old look.
